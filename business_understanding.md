Step 1: Business Understanding

1.1 Introduction

Overview / Background

Kore2 batteries are used to power electric motorcycles. The battery stores electrical energy that the motorcycle uses while riding. Recently, a significant number of customers using Kore2 batteries have reported two main problems: rapid battery drain and sudden motorcycle shutdowns without warning.

Rapid battery drain means that a battery loses its available energy faster than expected. This can reduce how far a motorcycle can travel before the battery needs to be charged. Abrupt shutdowns can also interrupt a rider's journey and affect their ability to work.

However, customer complaints alone cannot tell us whether the batteries are actually performing abnormally. The available battery performance data needs to be analyzed to determine whether the complaints are supported by evidence.

1.2 Business Problem

Kore2 customers are reporting that some batteries drain too quickly and may be associated with unexpected motorcycle shutdowns. At the moment, it is not clear whether the problem is caused by a small group of faulty batteries, the entire Kore2 battery batch, external factors, or customer reports that are not supported by the data.

This creates a problem for Kore2 because the Engineering and Customer Service teams need reliable evidence before deciding whether specific batteries should be investigated, repaired, replaced, or whether a wider issue should be examined.

1.3 Main Goal

The main goal of this project is to use battery performance data to determine whether Kore2 batteries are experiencing unusually fast energy drain or sudden shutdowns, identify any abnormal batteries, and recommend what action the company should take.

In simple terms: We want to find out whether the customers are right and, if they are, which batteries are causing the problem.

1.4 Objectives

Main Objective

To determine whether there are Kore2 batteries showing abnormal performance that could explain customer complaints about rapid battery drain and abrupt motorcycle shutdowns.

Specific Objectives

Identify abnormal batteries by comparing individual battery performance with the normal performance range.

Investigate rapid battery drain by examining how quickly different batteries lose available energy.

Investigate abrupt motorcycle shutdowns and determine whether they are associated with particular batteries.

Validate customer complaints by using statistical analysis to determine whether the reported problems are supported by the data.

Provide clear and practical recommendations to the Engineering and Customer Service teams based on the findings.

1.5 Key Questions

Are some Kore2 batteries draining faster than normal?

Which batteries, if any, have unusually high drain rates?

Are sudden motorcycle shutdowns associated with particular batteries?

Are the customer complaints supported by the battery performance data?

Is the problem limited to a small group of batteries or does it affect the wider Kore2 batch?

Could external factors explain the reported problems?

What action should Kore2 take based on the results?
